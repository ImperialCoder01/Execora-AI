# Hackathon Project Summary — Execora

* **Project Name**: Execora
* **One-liner**: From information to execution.
* **Problem**: Unstructured information in meeting notes, emails, and PRDs hides actionable work and leads to dropped commitments.
* **Solution**: AI extracts tasks, deadlines, decisions, and priorities, detects unassigned gaps, and turns them into a prioritized execution plan.
* **AI Engine**: Groq-powered LLaMA 3.3 70B structured extraction and zero-hallucination grounded reasoning.
* **Target Users**: Students, developers, project teams, engineering leads, professionals, and hackathon participants.
* **Core Impact**: Reduces by 90% the manual effort required to convert informal discussions into structured, executable work specifications.
