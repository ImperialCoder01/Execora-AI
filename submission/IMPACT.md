# Demonstrated Impact — ActionFlow AI

* **Efficiency Gain**: Reduces time spent turning meeting syncs into tasks from 30+ minutes down to 2 seconds.
* **Risk Prevention**: Automatically catches unassigned tasks (e.g. unassigned video creator) and vague deadlines before they cause project failure.
* **Execution Clarity**: Synthesizes a sequential, dependency-ordered Action Plan with human approval gates.
* **Reliable Truth**: Bounded Q&A engine prevents LLM hallucinations from misleading team members on dates or responsibilities.
